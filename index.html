About the Club

The Pre-Anesthesiologist Assistant Club is a student-led organization at Middle Tennessee State University that supports undergraduate students interested in pursuing careers as anesthesiologist assistants. The club provides a collaborative environment focused on academic excellence, professional development, and career preparation.

Our Purpose

The purpose of the Pre-Anesthesiologist Assistant Club is to provide students with educational, professional, and networking opportunities that support preparation for careers as anesthesiologist assistants, promote academic excellence, and foster mentorship, collaboration, and service within the healthcare community.

Club Activities

  • Guest speakers from anesthesia and healthcare professions
  • Graduate school and application preparation workshops
  • Networking opportunities with professionals and programs
  • Academic and peer mentorship
  • Community service and healthcare-related outreach

Membership

Membership is open to all MTSU students interested in learning more about the anesthesiologist assistant profession or preparing for graduate-level anesthesia programs. No prior experience is required.
